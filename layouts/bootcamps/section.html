We (Jess, Eda, and Carmen) have been running free bootcamps for a few years, and we're happy to be offering these through the Bad Website Club now!

Details

  • No signups, no mandatory attendance, no tests: this is completely free and paced as you like.
  • Materials and a free certificate are part of {{ .Params.fcc_name }} on freeCodeCamp.
  • You can chat and collaborate in our dedicated channel in the freeCodeCamp Discord server.

Where?

Jess, Eda, and Carmen host the bootcamp together. You can follow along on our YouTube channel and on Jess' Twitch channel and Carmen's Twitch channel.

Calendar?

Download or subscribe to the full session calendar. For Google Calendar, use the iCal feed with these subscription instructions.

Participation

This bootcamp is designed to be beginner friendly, and no programming experience is needed.

To participate in this bootcamp, you should be:

  • Able to connect to the internet
  • Able to use a web browser
  • Able to use search engines to look up new information

Therefore, the only requirements are:

  • Internet connection
  • Web browser

Note: Learners will be the most successful if they're able to study from a device with a physical keyboard instead of a phone or tablet.
