## Before this session, please:

- Read [Working with Audio and Video Elements: What Are the Roles of the HTML Audio and Video Elements, and How Do They Work?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-audio-and-video-elements/what-are-the-roles-of-the-html-audio-and-video-elements) and take notes
- Preview [Build an HTML Music Player](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-html-music-player/step-1) and try to work through the steps alone, recording questions and any new concepts
- Preview [Build an HTML Video Player](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-html-video-player/step-1) and try to work through the steps alone, recording questions and any new concepts
- Complete [Build an HTML Audio and Video Player](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-html-audio-and-video-player/build-an-html-audio-and-video-player) on your own

## During this session, we'll:

- Review notes
- We'll work through key steps from [Build an HTML Music Player](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-html-music-player/step-1) to better understand audio elements in HTML
- We'll work through key steps from [Build an HTML Video Player](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-html-video-player/step-1) to better understand video elements in HTML
- We'll talk through [Build an HTML Audio and Video Player](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-html-audio-and-video-player/build-an-html-audio-and-video-player) together to help prepare you to finish this on your own, over the next few days

- Read [Working with Images and SVGs: What Are Common Ways to Optimize Media Assets?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-images-and-svgs/what-are-common-ways-to-optimize-media-assets) and take notes
- Read [Working with Images and SVGs: What Are the Different Types of Image Licenses, and How Do They Work?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-images-and-svgs/what-are-the-different-types-of-image-licenses) and take notes
- Read [Working with Images and SVGs: What Are SVGs, and When Should You Use Them?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-images-and-svgs/what-are-svgs) and take notes
- Preview [Build a Heart Icon](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-build-a-heart-icon/step-1) and try to work through the steps alone, recording questions and any new concepts
