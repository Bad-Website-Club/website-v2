## Before this session, please:

- Read [Working with CSS Flexbox: What Is CSS Flexbox, and When Should You Use It?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-css-flexbox/what-is-css-flexbox) and take notes
- Read [Working with CSS Flexbox: What Are Some Common Flex Properties, and How Do They Work?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-css-flexbox/what-are-some-common-flex-properties) and take notes
- Try to complete all the steps from [Build a Flexbox Photo Gallery](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-flexbox-photo-gallery/step-1) to begin to get an understanding of flexbox
- Try to complete as much of [Design a Set of Colorful Boxes](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-colorful-boxes/step-1) as you can, to reinforce your understanding of flexbox. You may need to finish this over the next few days
- Complete [Design a Pricing Plans Layout Page](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-pricing-plans-layout/design-a-pricing-plans-layout-page) solo over the next few days
- Read [CSS Flexbox Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-css-flexbox/review-css-flexbox) and take notes
- Complete [CSS Flexbox Quiz](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/quiz-css-flexbox/quiz-css-flexbox) solo to test your knowledge of flexbox
- Spend the next few days or weeks completing [Build a Page of Playing Cards](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-page-of-playing-cards/build-a-page-of-playing-cards). Certification projects should show as much of your skill and style as possible.
