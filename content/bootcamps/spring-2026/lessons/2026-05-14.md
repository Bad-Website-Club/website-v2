## Before this session, please:

- Read [Working with Tables: What Are HTML Tables Used For, and What Should They Not Be Used For?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-tables/what-are-html-tables-used-for) and take notes
- Complete all steps from [Build a Final Exams Table](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-final-exams-table/step-1), recording any questions or new concepts introduced
- Please complete [Build a Book Catalog Table](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-book-catalog-table/build-a-book-catalog-table) over the next few days

## During this session, we'll:

- Review notes
- We'll work through key steps from [Build a Final Exams Table](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-final-exams-table/step-1) and talk about HTML tables in practice
- We'll talk through [Build a Book Catalog Table](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-book-catalog-table/build-a-book-catalog-table) to help prepare you to complete this lab solo

## After this session, please:

- Read [Working with HTML Tools: What Is an HTML Validator, and How Can It Help You Debug Your Code?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-html-tools/what-is-an-html-validator) and take notes
- Read [Working with HTML Tools: How to Use the DOM Inspector and DevTools to Debug and Build Your Projects](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-working-with-html-tools/how-to-use-the-dom-inspector-and-devtools) and take notes
- Read [HTML Tables and Forms Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-html-tables-and-forms/review-html-tables-and-forms) and take notes
- Complete [HTML Tables and Forms Quiz](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/quiz-html-tables-and-forms/quiz-html-tables-and-forms) solo to test your understanding
- Complete [Build a Survey Form](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-survey-form/build-a-survey-form) solo over the next week or so
