## Before this session, please:

- Read and take notes for:
  - [Debugging CSS: How Can You Use the DevTools Inspection Tool and CSS Validators to Debug Your CSS?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-debugging-css/how-can-you-use-the-devtools-inspection-tool)
  - [CSS Grid Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-css-grid/review-css-grid)
- Complete [Design a Newspaper Layout](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-newspaper-layout/design-a-newspaper-layout) solo over the next few days. You may want to write an article about something in your life, make up a story or use placeholder text for your news article.
- Complete [CSS Grid Quiz](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/quiz-css-grid/quiz-css-grid) solo to test your knowledge of CSS Grid
- Spend the next few days (or weeks!) completing [Build a Product Landing Page](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-product-landing-page/build-a-product-landing-page) solo. Don't forget that you can come back to improve this at a later date when you have more time.

## During this session, we'll:

- Review notes for:
  - [Debugging CSS: How Can You Use the DevTools Inspection Tool and CSS Validators to Debug Your CSS?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-debugging-css/how-can-you-use-the-devtools-inspection-tool)
  - [CSS Grid Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-css-grid/review-css-grid)
- We'll look at [Design a Newspaper Layout](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-newspaper-layout/design-a-newspaper-layout) together to prepare you to complete this lab on your own. We'll also talk about lorem ipsum
- We'll look at [Build a Product Landing Page](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-product-landing-page/build-a-product-landing-page) together to help prepare you to complete the certification project on your own.
