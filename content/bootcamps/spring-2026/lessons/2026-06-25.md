## Before this session, please:

- Read [Animations and Accessibility: What Are CSS Animations, and How Do They Work?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-animations-and-accessibility/what-are-css-animations) and take notes
- Read [Animations and Accessibility: What Are Accessibility Concerns Around Using Animations, and How Can prefers-reduced-motion Help?](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lecture-animations-and-accessibility/what-are-accessibility-concerns-around-using-animations) and take notes
- Try and complete all the steps from [Build an Animated Ferris Wheel](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-ferris-wheel/step-1)
- Complete [Build a Moon Orbit](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-moon-orbit/build-a-moon-orbit) solo over the next few days

## During this session, we'll:

- Review notes
- Review notes
- We'll look at key steps and concepts from [Build an Animated Ferris Wheel](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-ferris-wheel/step-1) together to review
- We'll look at [Build a Moon Orbit](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-moon-orbit/build-a-moon-orbit) together to understand what kinds of CSS animation you'll need to review to complete this lab

## After this session, please:

- Try to complete as many steps from [Build a Flappy Penguin](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-flappy-penguin/step-1) as possible before class. This workshop is Jess' favorite and she hopes you'll love it too.
- Complete [Build a Personal Portfolio](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-personal-portfolio/build-a-personal-portfolio) solo over the next few days. Even though this is a lab, we recommend you approach it like you would a certification. The work you do on this lab can be used as the basis of your own portfolio site to show off your skills!
- Read [CSS Animations Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-css-animations/review-css-animations) and take notes
- Complete [CSS Animations Quiz](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/quiz-css-animations/quiz-css-animations) solo to test your knowledge of CSS animations
