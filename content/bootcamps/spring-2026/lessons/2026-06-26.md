## Before this session, please:

- Read and take notes for:
  - [CSS Animations Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-css-animations/review-css-animations)
- Try to complete as many steps from [Build a Flappy Penguin](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-flappy-penguin/step-1) as possible before class. This workshop is Jess' favorite and she hopes you'll love it too.
- Complete [Build a Personal Portfolio](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-personal-portfolio/build-a-personal-portfolio) solo over the next few days. Even though this is a lab, we recommend you approach it like you would a certification. The work you do on this lab can be used as the basis of your own portfolio site to show off your skills!
- Complete [CSS Animations Quiz](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/quiz-css-animations/quiz-css-animations) solo to test your knowledge of CSS animations

## During this session, we'll:

- Review notes for:
  - [CSS Animations Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-css-animations/review-css-animations)
- We'll work through some of the steps from [Build a Flappy Penguin](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/workshop-flappy-penguin/step-1) together to share the joy of watching the penguin wave
- We'll talk through [Build a Personal Portfolio](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/lab-personal-portfolio/build-a-personal-portfolio) and discuss what you might want to include in your portfolio website

## After this session, please:

- Read and take notes for:
  - [CSS Review](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/review-css/review-css)
